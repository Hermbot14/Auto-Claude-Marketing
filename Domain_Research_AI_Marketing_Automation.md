## Trademark Conflict Checking

### Essential Checks Before Purchase:

1. **USPTO TESS** - https://tmsearch.uspto.gov/
   - Search "Advertising & Business" (Class 35)
   - Check for similar names in marketing software

2. **Google Trademark Search**
   - Query: `"DomainName" marketing software` OR `"DomainName" trademark`
   - Check first 3 pages of results

3. **Social Media Verification**
   - twitter.com/username
   - instagram.com/username
   - linkedin.com/company/username

4. **WIPO Global Brand Database** - https://www3.wipo.int/branddb/en/
   - International trademark search

### 🚩 Red Flags to Avoid:

- Existing marketing automation companies with similar names
- Names used by well-funded startups (even indirect competitors)
- Generic terms that are heavily trademarked:
  - "MarketingHub"
  - "CampaignPro"
  - "AutoMarket"
- Names similar to: Marketo, HubSpot, Pardot, Mailchimp, ActiveCampaign

## TLD Strategy

### Priority Order:
1. **.com** - Gold standard, most trust, best SEO
2. **.ai** - Perfect for AI companies, becoming industry standard
3. **.io** - Tech-friendly, recognized by developers
4. **.co** - Good .com alternative, widely accepted
5. **.xyz** - Trendy but less professional

### Avoid:
- .net (outdated for startups)
- .biz (unprofessional)
- .info (spammy reputation)
- .org (not for commercial entities)

### Pricing Expectations:
- **Standard .com:** $8-15/year
- **Premium .com:** $1,000 - $100,000+ (avoid for early-stage)
- **.ai:** $50-100/year (higher but worth it for AI companies)
- **.io:** $30-50/year
- **.co:** $10-20/year

## Decision Framework

### Score Each Domain On:
1. **Length** (shorter = better): /10
2. **Memorability** (easy to remember?): /10
3. **Spellability** (easy to spell after hearing?): /10
4. **Brandability** (distinctive, not generic?): /10
5. **SEO Value** (contains relevant keywords?): /10
6. **Trademark Risk** (low risk = high score): /10
7. **.com Availability** (available = high score): /10

### Calculate Total Score: /70
- **60-70:** Purchase immediately
- **50-59:** Strong contender
- **40-49:** Backup option
- **<40:** Skip

## Post-Purchase Checklist

After acquiring your domain:

- [ ] Enable WHOIS privacy protection
- [ ] Set up professional email (name@yourdomain.com)
- [ ] Secure social media handles:
  - twitter.com/yourdomain
  - instagram.com/yourdomain
  - linkedin.com/company/yourdomain
  - github.com/yourdomain (if open source)
- [ ] Set up SSL certificate (Let's Encrypt - free)
- [ ] Configure DNS records
- [ ] Set up domain auto-renewal
- [ ] Trademark the name (optional, consult attorney)
